# The Science

The apps let you work with real seismic data. These pages explain the science behind them:
what you are looking at on a seismogram, and why the method finds the quake. They are short
and build on each other, so read them in order the first time.

1. **[Seismic waves](waves.md):** what P and S waves are and how they move the rock.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Locator](../apps/wave-race.md), [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md), [Quake Waves](../apps/quake-waves.md).*
2. **[Reading a seismogram](seismograms.md):** how a seismometer records shaking, the parts of
   a quake record, and what else shows up (ocean hum, wind, people, blasts, distant quakes,
   instrument problems), with filters and spectrograms to pull them apart.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Locator](../apps/wave-race.md), [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md); School Pulse (coming soon).*
3. **[Distance from S minus P](s-minus-p.md):** how the time between P and S tells you how far
   away the quake was, and why the depth matters.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Locator](../apps/wave-race.md), [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md), [Quake Waves](../apps/quake-waves.md).*
4. **[Locating a quake](locating.md):** how distances from several stations pin down the
   epicenter, and how professionals do it.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Locator](../apps/wave-race.md), [Quake Waves](../apps/quake-waves.md).*
5. **[Travel times and Earth's layers](travel-times.md):** why arrivals line up on a record
   section and the slope is the speed, how a bend in the line reveals the crust's thickness,
   and how waves from far away dive through the whole Earth.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md).*
6. **[Frequencies, fading and sound](frequencies.md):** how any wiggle is a mix of notes, why
   the high notes fade first, how waves weaken with distance, and how to hear a quake.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md).*
7. **[Which way the ground moves](ground-motion.md):** the path the ground traces, proof that
   P pushes along its path and S shakes across it, and finding a quake's direction from one
   station.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md).*
8. **[Our stations and data](stations.md):** which seismometers the apps use, who runs them,
   and how to credit the data in your own work.<br>
   *Used in: [Quake Locator](../apps/wave-race.md), [Quake Explorer](../apps/quake-explorer.md).*
