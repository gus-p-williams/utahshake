UHSN Apps Utah High School Seismic Network

Guides, theory and teacher pages

These apps run in any browser, including Chromebooks. Nothing to install, no accounts. Until the school seismometers stream to UHSN, they use University of Utah stations near each school.

Wave Race

Pick the P and S waves of a real Utah earthquake at several stations, turn the time between them into distances, and find where the quake happened. Then compare with the University of Utah's answer.

Open Wave Race Student guide

Watch the Waves

A cartoon of P and S waves racing out from a quake to four stations. See the seismograms appear, and watch the S − P distances cross at the quake. A good warm-up for Wave Race.

Open the animation Locating a quake

School Pulse

See the daily rhythm of a school in its seismic noise: class changes, weekends, holidays.
