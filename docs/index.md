# UHSN Student Seismology

Apps and documentation for students and teachers in the **Utah High School Seismic
Network (UHSN)**, run by the Desert Research Institute.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Open the apps](app/index.html)**

    Runs in any browser, including Chromebooks. No install, no accounts.

- **[Wave Race](apps/wave-race.md)**

    Pick P and S waves on real Utah seismograms, turn them into distances, and locate the
    quake. Then check your answer against the University of Utah's.

- **Theory**

    [Seismic waves](theory/waves.md) · [Distance from S minus P](theory/s-minus-p.md) ·
    [Locating a quake](theory/locating.md)

- **Teachers**

    [Wave Race activity card](teachers/wave-race-card.md) (15-minute and full-period) ·
    [Standards crosswalk](teachers/standards.md)

</div>

!!! info "Data"
    Until the school Raspberry Shakes stream to UHSN, the apps use open data from
    University of Utah Seismograph Stations (network UU, via EarthScope) and the
    USGS earthquake catalog. See [Data sources](developers/data-sources.md).
